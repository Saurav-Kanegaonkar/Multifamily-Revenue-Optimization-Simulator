# Executive Findings

## What I Analyzed

I modeled an 18 asset multifamily portfolio with 5,100 units, 288 property-week operating rows, 288 comp-set observations, and 18 pricing recommendations.

## Findings

- Portfolio occupancy is 87.9 percent in the latest weekly snapshot, with five lease-up assets and several turnaround assets pulling down the blended rate.
- Weekly NOI is 13.6 percent below modeled budget, mainly because lower occupancy and concession pressure outweigh rent growth opportunities.
- The pricing queue separates easy approvals from assets requiring regional review or a pricing call, which mirrors the way a revenue analyst would avoid pushing rent growth where absorption risk is high.
- Live Oak Crossing is the top watchlist asset because exposure, occupancy gap, and NOI variance combine into the highest corrective-action score.

## Recommendation

Use the pricing approval queue for the weekly revenue-management call, then use the forecast watchlist to drive operating follow-up with regional property managers. The highest-value behavior is not a blanket rent increase. It is separating rent capture opportunities from assets where leasing velocity and exposure require a course correction.
